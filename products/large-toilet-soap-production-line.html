Large Toilet Soap Production Line

Full-scale toilet soap production line for high-volume manufacturers. Soap noodles are mixed with fragrance and color, refined, vacuum-plodded into a continuous bar, then automatically cut and stamped into finished toilet soap.

Key Features

  • High output for industrial-scale soap manufacturing
  • Vacuum plodding removes air for harder, smoother bars
  • Automatic cutting and stamping line with consistent bar weight
  • Refiner (conche) ensures uniform color and fragrance
  • Designed for 24-hour continuous operation

Applications

Large soap factories and OEM manufacturers producing toilet soap at scale.
