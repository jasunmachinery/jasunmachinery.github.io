Small Soap Noodles Production Line

Small-scale soap noodles production line: saponification, drying and plodding turn oils and caustic soda into dry soap noodles — the base material for toilet and laundry soap bars.

Key Features

  • Complete route from oil to soap noodles
  • Drying system controls noodle moisture
  • Plodding forms uniform noodles
  • Right-sized for small factories
  • Noodles feed directly into bar lines

Applications

Small factories making their own soap noodles for bar production.
